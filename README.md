# ♟️ Chess Stage

### *Move the light. Wake the board.*

An experimental interactive experience where **light becomes the interface for discovery**.

Instead of navigating through traditional pages, visitors explore a digital stage where illuminated chess pieces reveal different chapters of my work. Every movement of light uncovers another story, transforming a portfolio into an immersive experience.

<img src="./assets/chess-pieces.png" width="850" alt="Chess Stage Chess Pieces">

</div>

## Experience

Rather than clicking through menus, visitors can:

- Move a physical spotlight across the stage
- Reveal hidden chess pieces through light
- Discover projects through exploration instead of navigation
- Experience a portfolio inspired by interactive installations

Each chess piece acts as a metaphor for a different project, idea, or chapter in my design journey, encouraging curiosity over conventional navigation.

---

## Inspiration

Inspired by installation art, theatrical lighting, and immersive exhibitions, Chess Stage explores how interaction, atmosphere, and storytelling can create a more memorable way to present design work.

Originally inspired by an HTML-in-Canvas lighting experiment, the project has evolved into an original interactive portfolio experience with its own visual language and narrative.
